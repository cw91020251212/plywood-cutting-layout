    🪚 木材切割排版

    先新增部件，再新增原板，按「計算」產生候選排版。通常設定已足夠；需要時才展開更多板材設定、切割詳情或其他操作。

    功能一覽

    📏 單位輸入：公制使用米／厘米／毫米；英制使用尺／寸／分，長度、闊度及數量分開輸入。

    🪚 多種排版：可選鋸路方向、慳料或開料方便優先；結果會顯示利用率及使用板數。

    🔁 方案及微調：可查看上一／下一個已驗證候選、旋轉部件、移動部件、對齊，以及只重新排版指定板材。

    📋 詳情及輸出：可展開逐刀切割詳情、材料帳，儲存／載入 JSON，列印報告及儲存排版圖。

    📱 分享及安裝：設定內可分享連結；手機支援原生分享表，亦可安裝成 App。

    ⚙ 顯示設定：設定內可切換深色模式、調整字體，以及用 1 至 5 級調校右下角上下箭咀透明度。

    使用步驟

    1. 設定單位、鋸縫／鋸片厚度及需要的修邊值。

    2. 輸入部件長、闊和數量，按「新增」。

    3. 輸入原板長、闊和數量，按「新增」。

    4. 選擇排版目標及切割策略；不確定時沿用預設值。

    5. 按「計算」，在結果區查看排版圖、利用率、板數及逐刀詳情。

    6. 需要修改時，先按相應的微調功能；修改資料後重新計算並重新核對。

    支撐寬度設定

    在更多板材設定中輸入支撐寬度，可按該尺寸篩選材料；未知時可留空。開料前，核對板材、鋸縫、支撐及設備設定。

    🪚 Plywood Cutting Layout

    Add parts and sheets, then press Calculate to generate a candidate layout. The usual settings are enough for most jobs; expand the advanced sections only when needed.

    Features

    📏 Units: Metric uses metres, centimetres and millimetres; imperial uses feet, inches and fen. Length, width and quantity are separate inputs.

    🪚 Layout strategies: Choose the cutting direction and whether to prioritise fewer sheets or easier cutting. Results show utilisation and sheet count.

    🔁 Variations and adjustments: Review checked candidate variations, rotate or move parts, align parts, or rearrange one selected sheet without changing the others.

    📋 Details and output: Expand cut-by-cut details and material balance, save or load JSON, print a report, and save layout images.

    📱 Share and install: Share a link from Settings; supported phones open the native share sheet. The site can also be installed as an app.

    ⚙ Display settings: Settings includes dark mode, font-size controls, and five levels for the bottom scroll-arrow opacity.

    Steps

    1. Set units, kerf/blade thickness and any edge trims.

    2. Enter part length, width and quantity, then press Add.

    3. Enter sheet length, width and quantity, then press Add.

    4. Choose the layout objective and cutting strategy; keep the defaults if unsure.

    5. Press Calculate and review the layout, utilisation, sheet count and cut details.

    6. After any adjustment, calculate again and verify the new candidate.

    Support-width setting

    Enter a support width under More sheet settings to filter material dimensions, or leave blank if unknown. Before cutting, check the sheet, kerf, supports and equipment settings.
